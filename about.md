Michael builds sturdy outdoor furniture and home goods by hand: Adirondack chairs and sets, porch swings, gliders and rockers, farmhouse tables, planters, bird feeders, grill caddies, Texas tic-tac-toe boards, and seasonal decor for fall and Christmas.

Most pieces are pine, stained warm brown or left natural for you to finish. They're screwed together for strength, so they hold up on a Texas porch for years. It's the kind of craftsmanship that gets handed down through the generations.
